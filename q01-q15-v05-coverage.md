**Assessment date:** 2026-11-06  
**Corpus reviewed:** 43 accepted records from the 200-record NASM/NMAH convenience sample, using `build/corpus-selection-report-v05.json`. The other 157 sample records were checked for explicit question anchors and terms.

## Status definitions

- **Partial:** The selected slice contains relevant examples, but misses a required period, comparison group, entity, or causal link. It cannot yet answer the question as written.
- **Not covered:** No selected record supplies direct evidence for the central object, entity, or claim. This means absent from the current 200-record sample, not absent from the full Smithsonian collections.

## What this says about the current slice

The v0.5 slice is useful for early aviation control, guidance, electronics, electric watches, and typewriters. It does **not** yet support the Apollo/Gemini-centered questions that motivated much of Q01–Q12. Q09–Q15 have partial examples, but the missing endpoints and relationships prevent reliable answers as written.

The 200-record sample also does not contain the core Apollo/Gemini records needed to close these gaps. A search of the excluded sample found one incidental “Apollo” mention in the Ryan NYP “Spirit of St. Louis” record (`nasm_A19280021000`), but no direct AGC, Gemini, Buzz Aldrin, Raytheon/Apollo, redundancy/backup, or digital-computer coverage. This is a limitation of the convenience sample, not evidence that the full Smithsonian collections lack those records.

## Recommended next step

Keep policy v0.5 fixed while acquiring a small, targeted second sample for the missing anchors: AGC and suppliers; Gemini and Apollo onboard computing and guidance; Apollo 11 equipment and flown/test status; redundancy/backup evidence; and comparable consumer electronics with documented miniaturization or technology transfer. Then re-run the same question-coverage review against the expanded sample before choosing the V1 corpus size.
